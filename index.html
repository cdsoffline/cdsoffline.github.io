01 / What we do

Community, offline.

CDS Offline is a student organization dedicated to fostering community by forging connections through offline activities. Many students, especially in computer science and data science, spend much of their day online.

Our goal is to help facilitate in-person connections without the use of technology. We hold weekly events and activities, including board games, icebreakers, field trips to local parks, and more.
